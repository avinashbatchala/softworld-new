## Design Principles

### Monochromatic Advantages
- ✅ **Timeless**: Black and white never goes out of style
- ✅ **Professional**: Clean, sophisticated appearance
- ✅ **Accessible**: No color blindness concerns
- ✅ **Focused**: Content takes center stage
- ✅ **Versatile**: Easy to add brand colors later if needed

### shadcn/ui Inspiration
- Subtle gray gradients for depth
- Clean borders and shadows
- Minimal use of effects
- Focus on typography and spacing
- Glassmorphism for modern touch

## Visual Hierarchy

1. **White (#FFFFFF)**: Primary actions, headings, key content
2. **Light Gray (#A0A0A0)**: Body text, secondary content
3. **Medium Gray (#666666)**: Tertiary content, subtle text
4. **Dark Gray (#222222 - #333333)**: Borders, dividers
5. **Black (#0A0A0A - #1A1A1A)**: Backgrounds, surfaces
